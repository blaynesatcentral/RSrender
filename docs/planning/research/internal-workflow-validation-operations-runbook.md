# Internal workflow representative-validation operations runbook

**Issue:** GitHub #10, *Internal boring-log workflow and representative edge cases*  
**Status:** Operations authorized; human scheduling pending  
**Scope:** Recruitment, session execution, evidence handling, synthesis, saturation, and closure operations for the approved representative-validation protocol  
**Non-claim:** No participant contact, scheduling, consent, interview, artifact review, metadata extraction, recording, moderated task, or approved synthesis is claimed by this document.

## Operating decision

The product owner approved the six readiness categories requested for issue #10:

1. contact and recruitment authority;
2. availability of the required participant-function coverage;
3. the firm's notes and recording policy;
4. permitted artifact and anonymized-metadata access;
5. restricted evidence storage and retention; and
6. assignment of a sanitized-synthesis approver.

Names, contact routes, candidate identities, storage locations, access lists, retention periods, custodian records, and approver identities remain in the firm's approved non-public systems. Approval of these categories authorizes the human coordinator to begin recruitment through the approved internal route; it does not prove that any operational step has occurred.

Structured notes are the default. Audio, video, screen capture, automated transcription, meeting summaries, and AI note-taking remain off unless the firm separately authorizes the exact method and the participant separately consents at session start. Artifact viewing, copying, metadata extraction, quotation, and use of sanitized findings are distinct permissions.

## Governing evidence and vocabulary

This runbook executes, but does not replace:

- the [representative-validation protocol](internal-workflow-representative-validation-protocol.md), which defines claims RV-01 through RV-06, evidence fields, privacy rules, and closure gates;
- the [provisional workflow and edge-case report](internal-boring-log-workflow-edge-cases.md), which supplies hypotheses and fixture prompts rather than representative facts;
- the [human participation plan](../human-participation-plan.md), which separates human evidence, organizational authority, technical experiments, and product decisions; and
- the repository [ubiquitous language](../../../CONTEXT.md).

Use the established terms Log Project, Log Template, Log Set, Exploration, Reference Depth Range, Source Snapshot, Supplemental Source, Presentation Override, Refresh, Diagnostic, and Publication Audit. Capture a participant's own vocabulary in restricted notes before mapping its sanitized meaning to these terms.

## Roles and non-substitution

| Function | Operational responsibility | Cannot substitute for |
|---|---|---|
| Research sponsor/contact authority | Authorizes the recruitment route and organizational constraints. | Participant consent or evidence. |
| Human coordinator | Maintains the non-public roster, schedules sessions, protects identities, and operates the stopping rule. | Research synthesis, policy decisions, or product-owner acceptance. |
| Facilitator | Obtains separate permissions, conducts the approved session, and labels evidence. | Custodian approval or privacy approval. |
| Participant | Supplies voluntary evidence about functions they genuinely perform. | Product policy, statistical representativeness, or another function they do not perform. |
| Artifact/data custodian | Authorizes the exact viewing, copying, or metadata route for material they govern. | Participant consent or repository-release approval. |
| Evidence steward/synthesist | Controls restricted intake, provenance, sanitization derivatives, and contradiction ledgers. | Human privacy review or designated synthesis approval. |
| Human privacy reviewer | Verifies the exact release candidate contains no client data, production-response material, or re-identifying combinations. | Evidence sufficiency or product acceptance. |
| Sanitized-synthesis approver | Approves the exact privacy-safe release candidate. | Product-owner disposition of uncertainty or #10 closure. |
| Product owner | Accepts the final synthesis or a named uncertainty and its downstream restriction. | Missing consent, evidence, custodianship, or technical proof. |

A person may perform several functions, but each function, permission, evidence basis, and decision must be recorded separately. Management appointment, job title, participant preference, or majority count does not create product policy.

## Execution sequence

```text
Authority approved
  -> recruit and schedule six core sessions
  -> run and assess each core session
  -> verify function and variation coverage
  -> pause for core synthesis
  -> select one later challenge session
  -> assess it before selecting the next
  -> require two consecutive qualifying no-new-critical sessions
  -> complete privacy review and synthesis approval
  -> product-owner closure decision
  -> #16 may unlock only if every #10 closure gate passes
```

Do not schedule all possible sessions as one undifferentiated batch. The normally smallest defensible sequence is six core sessions, a synthesis pause, and at least two later sessions. A later session that exposes a new critical workflow, publication blocker, or fixture-changing edge case resets the consecutive no-new counter to zero.

## Stage gates

| Gate | Required result | Current state |
|---|---|---|
| G0 — governance | All applicable authority categories have named answers in approved non-public records. | Approved; details non-public. |
| G1 — core roster | Six candidates collectively target all required functions without assuming unproven role overlap. | Human coordinator action required. |
| G2 — core execution | Six countable individual 45–60 minute sessions, each with valid separate permissions. | Not started. |
| G3 — coverage | Two authors/formatters plus template, review/publication, data/laboratory, and IT/support functions are evidenced. | Not assessed. |
| G4 — core synthesis | Coverage, variation, novelty, contradictions, RV claims, and fixture consequences are classified. | Not started. |
| G5 — later challenge | One independently useful later candidate is selected against the current synthesis. | Not eligible. |
| G6 — saturation check | Two consecutive eligible later sessions add no critical workflow, publication blocker, or fixture-changing edge case. | Not eligible. |
| G7 — release and closure | Privacy review, synthesis approval, RV dispositions, contradiction handling, downstream obligations, and product-owner decision all pass. | Open. |

An unmet gate blocks only its dependent activity. It never authorizes an informal workaround, silent waiver, deletion, or claim of completion.

## Core-six recruitment plan

Recruit by functions actually performed, not title. One participant may receive credit for several functions only when they self-describe currently performing each function and provide relevant evidence for each. Genuine overlap never lowers the minimum of six countable sessions.

| Slot | Primary target | Evidence emphasis |
|---|---|---|
| C1 | Current boring-log author/formatter | A recent ordinary case, page composition, overflow, corrections, and handoffs. |
| C2 | Current author/formatter in a contrasting condition | Long/dense or unusual work, per-page Reference Depth Range, rework, and scale. |
| C3 | Log Template maintainer/approver | Template authority, variants, assignment scope, pagination, fonts/assets, and fit policies. |
| C4 | Reviewer/approver/issuer/downstream consumer | Review, publication blockers, Publication Audit needs, PDF acceptance, reissue, and consequences. |
| C5 | RSLog-data or laboratory-exchange steward | Source state, success/empty/failure, laboratory exchange, provenance, matching, Refresh, and correction boundaries. |
| C6 | Deployment/storage/recovery/user-support owner | Storage classes, concurrency, recovery, deployment/update practice, incidents, and support evidence. |

The coordinator may reorder slots for availability. If role overlap fills a cell early, retarget the freed emphasis to an unrepresented function, variation, contradiction, or context while retaining six sessions.

Across the sample, deliberately seek evidence about:

- simple/short and long/dense Log Sets;
- single-template and mixed Template Assignment practice;
- first, continuation, and last-page behavior;
- per-page Reference Depth Ranges;
- text overflow, presentation correction, and Refresh conflict;
- laboratory moisture, plastic limit, and liquid limit when used;
- differing review/publication practices; and
- differing storage, concurrency, recovery, or support conditions.

These are sampling targets, not assumed firm practices. Classify each after evidence as covered, evidenced absent, unrepresented, not applicable, or explicitly accepted uncertainty.

## Human coordinator action packet

The authorized coordinator now performs these steps in the approved non-public system:

1. Map candidate availability to C1–C6 and the variation targets without placing identities in the repository or GitHub.
2. Confirm each candidate's likely functions through the approved route; final credit still depends on session evidence.
3. Send the privacy-safe invitation below to six core candidates and maintain replacements for declines or cancellations.
4. Schedule individual 45–60 minute sessions. Do not predesignate core sessions as saturation checks.
5. Return only the sanitized aggregate status format defined near the end of this runbook.

### Invitation template

Use only in an approved non-public communication channel.

> **Subject: Voluntary RSrender boring-log workflow research session**
>
> You are invited to a voluntary 45–60 minute session about how boring-log work is currently performed. The study is about the work, not your performance. We want to understand real sequences, handoffs, document variations, exceptions, review needs, and scale.
>
> The planned method is structured notes only, with no audio, video, or screen recording. Any later recording request would be separate and optional. You may decline any question, decline any artifact request, request a different input/display/pacing/communication format, or stop at any time.
>
> Do not prepare or share a production file. Please do not show or state credentials, client or project identities, coordinates, production values or prose, meaningful notes, filenames, paths, API material, or other identifying information. Any artifact viewing would require separate approval and permission and would not authorize copying or metadata extraction.
>
> If you are interested, respond through the approved internal scheduling route with availability only; do not include client or project information.

### Scheduling confirmation template

> Thank you for agreeing to a voluntary RSrender workflow session. The session is 45–60 minutes and uses structured notes only; recording is off. No preparation or file sharing is required. If helpful, think of one recent boring-log deliverable and the actions from request through issued PDF or reissue, without identifying the client or project. Participation remains voluntary, and artifact viewing would be requested separately.

### Optional recording addendum

Do not use this addendum unless the firm has approved the exact recording type, purpose, restricted access, retention, and disposition. State that the session can proceed with notes only, that declining does not affect participation, and that a pre-session “yes” merely permits asking again. Recording begins only after explicit session-day consent. A blank answer is `No`.

## Per-session controls

### Before the session

- Create a random, non-meaningful `sessionId` in restricted storage; do not derive it from a person, title, office, date, project, artifact, or scheduling slot.
- Verify the contact route, structured-note authority, restricted storage, retention/disposition route, incident route, and synthesis approver.
- Verify separately approved artifact-viewing, copying, metadata, quotation, or recording routes; otherwise mark each `Not requested`.
- Disable recording, transcription, meeting-summary, and AI-notetaking features by default.
- Hide unrelated sensitive content and ensure the participant's requested input, display, pacing, or communication format is available.

### Opening and permissions

Tell the participant the session studies work rather than performance; participation is voluntary; any question or artifact may be declined; and they may stop at any time. Repeat the prohibited-content boundary.

Record `Yes`, `No`, or `Not requested` separately for:

- participation;
- structured notes;
- audio recording;
- video/screen capture;
- artifact viewing;
- artifact copying;
- metadata extraction;
- direct quotation; and
- possible use of approved sanitized findings.

A blank is `No`. If participation or structured notes is `No`, do not proceed under this notes-based protocol. Firm permission does not replace participant consent, and participant consent does not replace custodian authority.

### Session route

Follow the participant's real sequence and use the detailed prompts in the governing protocol:

1. context and permissions — about 3 minutes;
2. recent-case reconstruction — about 12 minutes;
3. variation, failures, and corrections — about 10 minutes;
4. template and pagination workflow — about 8 minutes;
5. source, Refresh, Supplemental Source, and local Presentation Override practice — about 8 minutes;
6. review, publication, storage, and recovery — about 8 minutes;
7. scale and frequency — about 5 minutes; and
8. wrap-up and missing perspectives — about 3 minutes.

Ask for actions before opinions. Keep current practice separate from desired RSrender behavior, frequency from professional consequence, participant report from direct observation, recalled values from measured or custodian aggregates, and synthetic reactions from occurrence evidence. An interview preference may raise a policy question; it does not settle one.

### Evidence labels

Label each substantive item as participant report, direct observation, measured metadata, custodian aggregate, approved procedure, synthetic review, hypothesis, or policy question. Preserve `unknown`, `not_available`, `not_applicable`, and `not_permitted`; never encode missing as zero.

Trace sanitized candidate findings to the applicable RV claim, provisional workflow, `EC-*` edge case, `FX-*` fixture family, frequency basis, professional consequence, contradiction state, and potential downstream requirement.

### Unexpected prohibited content

Stop the affected viewing or capture immediately, do not transcribe or reproduce the content, and invoke the approved non-public incident/disposition route. This runbook grants no deletion authority.

### Within one working day

- Mark a session countable only when participation and required evidence handling were valid.
- Complete its novelty review and update function/variation coverage from evidence rather than recruitment labels.
- Preserve competing accounts separately; do not decide a contradiction by vote.
- Route missing metadata, artifact, policy, prototype, or function evidence to its actual owner.
- Keep participant-level records and `sessionId` values out of the repository and GitHub.

## Core synthesis and later-session rule

After six countable core sessions, pause scheduling of later checks and verify:

- six distinct countable core sessions are complete;
- two current authors/formatters and every other required function are evidenced;
- any multi-function credit passed the genuine-overlap test;
- variation targets are classified rather than left blank;
- each core session has a novelty review;
- workflows, publication blockers, edge cases, and fixture consequences have been updated in restricted synthesis;
- material contradictions are resolved, contextualized, routed, or explicitly open; and
- the next candidate is selected to challenge the synthesis rather than repeat it for convenience.

If function coverage is incomplete, run targeted core-remediation sessions and repeat the checkpoint. These are not saturation checks.

Run later sessions sequentially:

| Result | Counter action | Required follow-up |
|---|---:|---|
| No new critical workflow, publication blocker, or fixture-changing edge case | Increment by one. | Select the next independent challenge only after assessing this session. |
| Any stopping-rule novelty | Reset to zero. | Update synthesis, contradictions, coverage, and fixtures before selecting another challenge. |
| Session not countable or conducted before core synthesis | No change. | Replace it; it cannot be reclassified retroactively. |

Two consecutive qualifying later sessions are required. Reaching session ten is not automatic closure. If the rule remains unmet, keep #10 open, expand recruitment, or route the precise unrepresented segment for an explicit product-owner uncertainty decision with consequences and downstream restrictions.

## Restricted evidence handoff

Use four evidence zones:

| Zone | Contents | Repository/GitHub |
|---|---|---:|
| Restricted raw | Consent controls, pseudonymous notes, separately approved recordings/artifacts/metadata, minimum identity map if required. | Never. |
| Restricted working | Sanitization derivatives, finding ledger, contradiction register, proposed aggregates and revisions. | Never. |
| Approved release candidate | Exact aggregate/non-client-specific synthesis awaiting final approval. | Not until approved. |
| Committed synthesis | Approved findings, limitations, revised workflows/workloads, edge ranking, fixture changes, contradiction summary, and downstream obligations. | Yes. |

Every restricted handoff records non-meaningful control identifiers, collection route, evidence basis, permissions, custody, privacy class, observation scope, confidence, value state, units, RV/workflow/edge/fixture trace, contradiction state, and intake/release status. Restricted acceptance proves admissibility for analysis only; it does not prove truth, frequency, representativeness, usability, policy, or permission to commit.

Create a separate sanitization derivative rather than overwriting raw evidence. Remove identities, dates, locations, project/source identifiers, filenames, paths, production prose/values/assets, credentials, API/support material, reversible tokens, and re-identifying combinations. Express storage and laboratory evidence only through approved controlled categories. Automated scans may assist but cannot replace human privacy review.

## Synthesis and contradiction rules

Synthesize RV-01 through RV-06 separately:

- RV-03 requires approved metadata; interview recall alone cannot establish the workload envelope.
- RV-05 requires moderated task evidence at measured sizes; interface preferences alone cannot establish usability.
- Preserve direct, measured, aggregate, recalled, procedure, and synthetic evidence bases separately.
- Preserve rare, negative, and contradictory evidence rather than averaging it away.
- Classify competing accounts as contextual variation, evidence-basis mismatch, terminology mismatch, policy question, or material conflict.
- Route policy to the authorized owner; do not decide it by participant count.
- A material unresolved contradiction affecting a critical workflow, publication blocker, or fixture prevents the saturation rule and #10 closure unless the product owner explicitly accepts the uncertainty and downstream restriction.

The exact release candidate requires a human zero-client-data and no-production-response review plus approval from the designated synthesis approver. Any subsequent content change creates a new release candidate and requires renewed review.

## Closure contract

Issue #10 may close as representative validation complete only when:

1. authority and consent are documented for every performed activity;
2. the minimum sample, complete functional coverage, and stopping rule pass, or every missing segment has an explicit uncertainty decision;
3. RV-01 through RV-06 are each `Evidenced` or `Uncertainty explicitly accepted`;
4. material contradictions are resolved, contextualized, or explicitly accepted with consequences;
5. workflows, actor lenses, workload bands, edge rankings, and fixture requirements are revised or explicitly retained as hypotheses;
6. evidence provenance is not overstated;
7. no participant preference silently becomes product policy;
8. the exact synthesis passes human privacy review and designated approval;
9. raw/working evidence has a recorded non-public retention/disposition state; and
10. every downstream restriction and test obligation is carried into #16 and the affected product/acceptance specifications.

Until all gates pass, #10 stays open and #16 stays blocked.

### Closure attestation

The final repository-safe attestation records only:

- validation period at an approved coarse granularity;
- countable session count, represented function categories, variation categories, and unrepresented segments;
- stopping-rule result and whether two consecutive later checks qualified;
- RV-01–RV-06 status, evidence basis, limitation, or accepted uncertainty;
- open material contradiction count and sanitized dispositions;
- references to revised workflows, workloads, edge rankings, and approved fixture changes;
- human privacy/no-production-response review results;
- approving function and a non-identifying control reference;
- raw/working evidence disposition state, without location, duration, person, or method;
- product-owner decision; and
- #16 and downstream obligations.

It never contains identities, contact details, session IDs, consent records, raw notes, recordings, artifacts, participant-level metadata, production material, paths, or restricted policy details.
